Piano Touch Game

Play real songs on a two octave piano. Notes fall, you play along.

1

Hit the line

Press the piano key when its tile reaches the glowing line. The closer to the beat, the better your score.

2

Hold the long ones

Tall tiles are long notes. Keep the key held down until the whole tile has passed the line.

3

Beat your best

Pick a song and a difficulty. Your best score for each one is saved to the leaderboard.

Best with headphones. If the music feels out of step with the tiles, nudge the Audio Offset slider on the next screen.
